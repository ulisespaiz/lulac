Council #2055

Issues

Council #2055 highlights six issue areas that reflect LULAC’s broader mission in the Salinas community.

Our Key Issue Areas

Six areas named by Council #2055

These areas align with the Council’s long-standing focus on supportive services, development programs, community strength, and leadership.

Economic Advancement

Economic opportunity, employment, and stronger pathways for community prosperity.

Education

Educational attainment, access to learning, scholarships, and opportunities for students.

Political Engagement

Civic participation and a stronger community voice in public life.

Housing

Housing conditions and access that affect Hispanic families and communities.

Healthcare

Community health, healthcare access, and useful health information and resources.

Civil Rights

Equal treatment, opportunity, and protection of civil rights for Hispanic communities.
