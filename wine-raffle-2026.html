Scholarship fundraiser

Wine Raffle 2026

Three winners this year. Every ticket goes to the LULAC #2055 Scholarship Fund for the Class of 2027. Winners are announced Saturday, December 12, 2026 at 4:00 PM on Facebook Live.

Raffle details

1st prize
25 bottles of wine, each valued at $25 and up, plus a wine fridge.
2nd prize
10 bottles of wine.
3rd prize
5 bottles of wine.
Delivery
Free delivery to all three winners.
Tickets
$20 each, or 6 for $100.
Drawing
Saturday, December 12, 2026, 4:00 PM, live on Facebook. You do not need to be present to win.
Buy with Venmo Call or text Chris

Where the money goes

This year Council #2055 awarded 75 scholarships worth more than $46,000 to graduating seniors going on to higher education. The council serves all nine Salinas-area high schools. The raffle funds next year's awards for the Class of 2027.

This is a scholarship fundraiser. We wish everyone could win. Thank you for supporting our students.

Sponsored by MP Express and Team Barrera, Windermere Real Estate.
