Mission

Economic advancement, education, political influence, housing, health, and civil rights.

LULAC works to advance the economic condition, educational attainment, political influence, housing, health, and civil rights of the Hispanic population of the United States.

Council #2055

Serving Salinas since 1973.

Since 1973, Council #2055 has organized supportive services and development programs designed to build strong communities, professional networks, and leadership in both the public and private sectors of the Salinas community.

The Council website identifies Council #2055 as a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization and states that donations are tax-deductible.

National LULAC Association

Part of a nationwide LULAC network.

National LULAC is described by Council #2055 as the largest and oldest Hispanic organization in the United States, advancing education, economic opportunity, political influence, housing, health, and civil rights through more than 1,000 community-based councils nationwide.
